# E-commerce Sales Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data using Python to understand sales performance, revenue trends, customer behavior, and product category performance.

The project includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), KPI calculation, data visualization, and business recommendations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Clean and prepare the raw e-commerce dataset.
- Identify and handle missing values and duplicate records.
- Calculate important business KPIs.
- Analyze monthly sales and revenue trends.
- Understand customer ratings and delivery performance.
- Compare revenue across product categories and regions.
- Create visualizations and a sales dashboard mockup.
- Provide meaningful, data-driven business recommendations.

## 📂 Dataset Information

The dataset used in this project is `ecommerce_sales_analytics_5000.csv`.

It contains e-commerce sales records with information such as:

- Order ID
- Order Date
- Customer ID
- Product Category
- Region
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Discount
- Payment Method
- Delivery Days
- Customer Rating
- Revenue

## 🧹 Data Cleaning

The data preparation process includes:

- Checking for missing values.
- Identifying and removing duplicate records.
- Converting order dates into the correct date format.
- Standardizing category, region, and payment method names.
- Converting numerical columns into appropriate data types.
- Validating numerical values.
- Saving the cleaned dataset separately from the original data.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The project calculates the following business metrics:

- **Total Revenue:** Total revenue recorded in the dataset.
- **Total Orders:** Number of unique orders.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** Total revenue divided by the number of unique orders.
- **Total Units Sold:** Total quantity of products sold.
- **Top-Selling Category:** Product category with the highest quantity sold.
- **Top Revenue-Generating Category:** Product category generating the highest revenue.

## 📈 Data Visualization

The project includes the following visualizations:

1. **Revenue by Category** – Compares revenue generated by different product categories.
2. **Monthly Revenue Trend** – Shows changes in revenue over time.
3. **Payment Method Analysis** – Displays the share of orders by payment method.
4. **Revenue by Region** – Compares sales performance across regions.
5. **Sales Dashboard Mockup** – Presents important KPIs and charts in one view.

## 👥 Customer Insights

Customer analysis focuses on:

- Average customer ratings.
- Ratings across product categories.
- Average delivery time.
- Delivery performance by region.
- Customer purchase frequency.
- Payment method preferences.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the results of the analysis, businesses can:

- Identify high-performing product categories.
- Review sales fluctuations and investigate unusual changes.
- Improve inventory planning based on product demand.
- Investigate regions with longer delivery times.
- Monitor customer ratings to identify areas for improvement.
- Evaluate payment method preferences when optimizing checkout options.

Recommendations should be finalized after reviewing the actual results and visualizations.

## ⚠️ Project Limitations

- The dataset contains product categories but does not include individual product names. Therefore, individual product-level revenue rankings cannot be calculated.
- The findings describe the available dataset and do not independently establish the causes of sales trends or customer behavior.
- The accuracy of the analysis depends on the quality and completeness of the supplied data.
